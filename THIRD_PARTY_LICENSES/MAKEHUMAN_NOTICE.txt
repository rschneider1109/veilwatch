MakeHuman HM08 / MPFB data foundation
======================================

Veilwatch Character Forge v2 uses selected MakeHuman HM08 data files sourced
from the MPFB 2.0.17 / MakeHuman Community asset ecosystem as its browser-native
human foundation.

The included basemesh identifies itself as CC0 and retains its original
copyright/license header in:

  api/public/assets/characters/makehuman/runtime/base.obj

Veilwatch does not bundle or execute Blender or the MPFB Blender add-on at
runtime. The browser runtime in api/src/3d/makehuman_runtime.mjs consumes the
compatible HM08 basemesh, rig, weights, target data, fitted .mhclo-derived asset
packs, facial targets, visemes, materials, and pose data directly.

Section 5 also includes the user-supplied MakeHuman Community Faceunits 01,
Visemes 01, Visemes 02, MakeHuman System Poses, Poses 01, Poses 03, and Poses 05
source data in compact browser-runtime packs. Original per-asset licensing data
from the source packages should be retained with the project's source-asset
archive when attribution is required by a particular upstream asset.

Legacy Vitruvian assets remain in the repository only as a load fallback while
the MakeHuman migration is being completed and validated.
